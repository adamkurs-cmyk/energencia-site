Meet Adam

I found this work because I needed it myself.

After years in the corporate world, I knew I wanted something that felt more human. Reiki was my first step. Soon after, I had a dream that I was giving someone a Sound Massage. At the time, I didn't even know such a practice existed. The next morning I searched for it. It was real.

I've now worked one-to-one with more than 350 people across Berlin, Tulum and Valencia. My work isn't about fixing people — it's about creating a space where your body can stop holding everything for a while.

What should I wear?+

For Sound Massage, wear comfortable natural fabrics without metal (zips, belts or jewellery). For every session, avoid a heavy meal for around two hours beforehand, and drink plenty of water.

Is it suitable for everyone?+

Please let me know before booking if you have a pacemaker, epilepsy, certain heart conditions, are in the first trimester of pregnancy, or experience psychosis or schizophrenia. None of this is medical treatment, and it doesn't replace it.

Where are sessions held?+

In my studio in Valencia. I'll send you the address after booking.

How do I book?+
